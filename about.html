Our Story

Where Passion Meets Visual Perfection

Founded at the heart of Veppur on Main Road, Venus Studio was created with a single mission: to provide families, couples, institutions, and businesses with world-class photography and premium gifting under one welcoming roof.

What began as a dedicated photography space has expanded into a full-scale creative production house. Today, we craft custom-printed keepsakes, luxury wedding photobooks, 4K multi-camera wedding films, and large-format flex branding for festivals and corporate gatherings.
